# Privacy policy

PDF Unlock does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- The PDFs you open and the passwords you type stay on your computer. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- The app has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, ads or tracking.
- The app doesn't connect to the internet. (The installer from GitHub downloads qpdf from its official GitHub release during installation. The Microsoft Store version already includes qpdf.)
- The app only reads the PDF you choose and writes the unlocked copy where you choose to save it.

If you have questions, [open an issue](https://github.com/joaomvfsantos/pdf-password-remover/issues) on GitHub.
