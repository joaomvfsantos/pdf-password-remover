# Microsoft Store listing (English)

Copy each block into the matching field in Partner Center under **Store listings > English (United States)**. The limit for each field is in its heading, and every text below fits it.

## Description (required, 10,000 characters)

```
Tired of typing the same password every time you open a PDF? PDF Unlock saves an unlocked copy of the file, so it opens straight away next time.

Bank statements, payslips, tax documents, utility bills and insurance letters often arrive password-protected. If you know the password, PDF Unlock removes it in a couple of clicks.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose the locked PDF.
2. Type its password.
3. Click Decrypt and choose where to save the unlocked copy.

That's it. The new file opens in any PDF reader without asking for a password.

WHY PDF UNLOCK
• Simple: one small window, no settings to figure out.
• Private: everything happens on your PC. Your files and passwords are never uploaded anywhere, and the app works without an internet connection.
• Safe: your original file is never changed. The app saves a separate copy, named "<name> (unlocked).pdf" by default.
• No accounts, ads, subscriptions or tracking.
• Free and open source.

PLEASE NOTE
You need to know the PDF's password. PDF Unlock does not guess, crack or bypass passwords. Only unlock documents that you own or are allowed to use.

PDF Unlock uses qpdf (https://qpdf.sourceforge.io/), licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
```
